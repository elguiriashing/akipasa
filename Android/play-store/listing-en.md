# Google Play listing (en-US default)

## Name

AkiPasa

## Short description

Discover events, venues and things to do near you in Spain.

## Full description

Discover what is happening near you with AkiPasa.

Explore events, venues and things to do in Spain. Filter by date, category,
accessibility and price, save favourites, and follow the places that interest
you. Check each event's details, open its official booking page, and switch
between Spanish and English.

With an AkiPasa account, you can also take part in the community, earn progress
and rewards through valid visits, and manage your preferences and privacy.
Approved businesses have tools to manage their venues, events and content on
the same platform.

AkiPasa for Android uses the same service, account and content as akipasa.com,
so your data and changes stay in sync.

Some features require sign-in. Location is optional and requested only after a
clear action; you can always choose an area manually.
