# AkiPals 3D model provenance and licence

The 3D model assets in this folder were obtained from the CC0
**Quaternius Universal Base Characters, Standard (free) pack**.
They are independent of AkiPasa's authored studio, wardrobe and code.

- `akipals-base-female.glb`: the `Superhero_Female_FullBody.gltf`
  model converted to one GLB by BeefTV's free-standard preparation pipeline,
  committed at [BeefTV asset](https://github.com/glanderness/BeefTV/blob/a7f099c3bf3c28aff0ffb9a30ead77e3733d0ca4/web/public/canvas/models/quaternius-standard-female.glb).
- `akipals-base-male.glb`: same free Standard pack's
  `Superhero_Male_FullBody.gltf`, converted and redistributed by BeefTV.
- Quaternius' official asset page:
  https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
- Quaternius' official free standard download:
  https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters
- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- The intermediary project's source and licence statements:
  https://github.com/glanderness/BeefTV/blob/main/web/public/canvas/models/quaternius-standard-female.LICENSE.md
  https://github.com/glanderness/BeefTV/blob/main/web/public/canvas/models/quaternius-standard-male.LICENSE.md

The prebuilt GLBs have body rigs and geometry but **do not include** the
fashionable clothes, hair or high-quality facial morph targets shown in AkiPals
concept-art mockups. The AkiPals studio's original procedural hair and clothes
are separate, real 3D mesh overlays. The images used in the visual target
are _concept only_, NOT textures/mesh sources.

These GLBs require production art approval and optimisation before public
release; they are engineering staging assets, not final professional 3D avatars.
Any commercial branded garments need licensing from their owners.
