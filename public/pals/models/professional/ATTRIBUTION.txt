Modified for AkiPals: baked standing poses and production modifiers, removed helper/control geometry,
reduced geometry and textures for mobile, adapted authored materials to glTF PBR, and normalized scale.
These models use the shared Three.js GLB renderer; the full M3 CharacterStudio engine is not included.
Source archive, Blender source and GLB SHA-256 hashes are recorded in manifest.json.

The November 2026 Wardrobe additionally applies reversible runtime hair/garment shaping
and per-surface colour palettes. The original exported GLB files and their recorded hashes remain unchanged.
