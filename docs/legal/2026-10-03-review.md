## Release requirements

1. **Operator identity — company officer/adviser:** Confirm actual contracting entity while in formation, registry status and particulars when issued, and a public customer-service telephone. Test delivery and human handling of support, privacy and legal mailboxes. Do not invent a DPO or arbitration affiliation.

2. **Processing map — privacy lead/engineering:** Record actual fields, sources, purposes, bases and recipients for accounts, profiles, creators, imported listings, location/check-ins, rewards, payments, analytics, support and business modules. Complete processing records and relevant legitimate-interest assessments. Assess DPO, DPIA and minors requirements. The proposed minimum account age and guardian/contract-capacity handling need owner approval and implementation.

3. **Suppliers/transfers — privacy lead/engineering:** Reconcile deployed Cloudflare, Supabase, Resend, Google, Stripe, mapping and AI services with actual contracts, entities, roles, countries, retention and subprocessors. Verify DPAs, adequacy coverage or correct SCC modules, assessments and supplementary measures. An EU database region is not a complete transfer assessment.

4. **Cookies/storage — engineering/privacy lead:** Capture fresh-browser behaviour before a decision, after rejecting optional purposes, after granular choices and after withdrawal. Cover home, map, authentication, account, checkout and subdomains. Inventory actual names/patterns, domains, providers, purposes, durations and storage types. Verify equal access to accept/reject/configure, no premature optional SDKs/identifiers and functioning withdrawal. Historical pre-consent concerns remain unverified, not fixed by this PR. The reviewed client gates tracking with ak_personalisation=granted and queues under akipasa:behaviour-queue:v1; the queue is bounded to 100 entries, not by age. Establish appropriate client/server retention and test offline retries, withdrawal and erasure. Cookie-writing scope/lifetime remain unverified.

5. **Location/motion — engineering/privacy lead:** Trace network requests and stored records before claiming coordinates or sensors stay on-device. Explain check-in purposes and recipients. Separate visual effects, location, cookie preferences and browser permissions. Verify static/reduced-motion alternatives.

6. **Imported data/media — content lead/privacy adviser:** Determine personal-data scope, including sole-trader contacts. Assess Article 14 information timing and actual exemptions; a public policy is not a universal substitute. Document image/data licences and attribution; public availability is not permission. Correct/remove inaccurate or unlawfully sourced material.

7. **Marketing — marketing/privacy lead:** Record specific permission or a genuinely applicable existing-customer exception. Test unsubscribe and suppression. Public business contacts and Terms acceptance do not automatically authorise promotional email.

8. **Paid contracting — product/billing engineering:** Verify actual products, consumer tax-inclusive totals, billing periods, renewal/trial terms, technical requirements and unambiguous payment action. Keep versioned acceptance evidence and durable contractual copies. Test confirmations, zero-cost promotions, payment-without-access recovery, cancellation and refunds. Do not silently rewrite existing contracts.

9. **Withdrawal — consumer-law adviser/engineering:** Separate stopping renewal from statutory withdrawal. Implement correct early-performance requests and any applicable digital-content consent/acknowledgement. First login does not fully perform a continuing monthly service. Provide the model notice and an effective request route. Directive (EU) 2023/2673 sets a 19 June 2026 application date for national measures; exact Spanish transposition and the applicable online withdrawal-interface implementation were not established in this review and require verification.

10. **DSA/moderation — legal/trust and safety:** Classify the service and actual micro/small-enterprise exemptions. Test notices, reasons, proportionate restrictions and human redress. Confirm contact points, automation disclosures and required reporting. The draft's at-least-six-month review commitment needs operational adoption. Small size does not remove every intermediary/hosting duty.

11. **Business intermediation — product/business-law adviser:** Determine P2B scope. Verify main ranking parameters and relative importance, paid influence, own-offer treatment, distribution, data access and exit. Implement applicable durable notices, 15-day changes and 30-day whole-service termination rules, subject only to lawful exceptions. Assess complaints/mediator requirements against real company size.

12. **AkiHQ processing agreement — company officer/privacy/engineering:** Complete Annex A (customer, modules, subjects/fields, instructions, return/deletion), B (verified security and incidents), and C (actual authorised subprocessors, countries and safeguards). Obtain effective contractual incorporation before relevant processing. Test tenant separation, privileged access, exports, deletion and backups. Drafting an agreement does not pass a security audit.

13. **SaaS switching — legal/engineering:** Assess AkiHQ under Data Act Chapter VI. Complete pre-contract switching/exit information and actual capability: exportable data/assets, interfaces, formats, assistance, continuity, notice/transition/retrieval periods, deletion and permitted charges. Generic GDPR portability or vague export wording is not a substitute.

14. **AI — AI product owner/legal:** Assess actual provider/deployer roles, support interaction disclosure, training/retention and automated moderation. Implement applicable AI Act Article 50 information at the interaction/output point, not only in Privacy. Assess GDPR Article 22 separately. Not every AI Act obligation shares one applicability date.

15. **Accessibility — product/reviewer:** Assess Law 11/2023 e-commerce scope and genuine microenterprise exemptions. Audit keyboard, assistive technology, contrast, cookies, authentication, payment, cancellation and mobile/reduced-motion alternatives. Do not claim WCAG certification from CSS alone.

16. **Accommodation — tourism/platform-law adviser:** Establish whether AkiDuermo is discovery/outbound links or facilitates rentals/bookings. Assess relevant tourism, trader, consumer and tax-platform duties against the actual role. Recheck current rental-registry law: the Supreme Court judgment of 19 May 2026, BOE-A-2026-12300, annulled specified provisions of RD 1312/2024. Do not copy an unchanged 2025 national-registration checklist. Assess EU Regulation 2024/1028 and regional rules separately.

17. **POS/fiscal — fiscal software lead/adviser:** Determine actual invoicing-system scope and released version. Verify required functionality, records and producer declaration of responsibility where applicable. Terms or disclaimers do not certify VERI*FACTU or electronic-invoicing compliance. Recheck current AEAT guidance rather than historical deadlines.

18. **Future products — product/legal:** Paid virtual items, random rewards, cash-value benefits, competitions, direct ticketing and new booking/payment roles need offer-specific review. General clauses do not authorise every future model. Preserve confidentiality of unannounced features in public documents.
