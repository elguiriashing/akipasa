# AkiPasa brand and retention direction — 28 October 2026

## Position
**AkiPasa: ¿Qué hacemos hoy?** The mobile-first place to find a genuine plan with friends, tonight or this weekend. Spain-first brand, initial supply pilot Fuengirola, Mijas Costa and Benalmádena; expand city by city when event density warrants it. Core current audience: Spain-based 18–35-year-olds including residents, university-age adults, young workers and new arrivals. Don't stereotype or exclude older people. Future audience should be earned through age-appropriate discovery, not marketing/tracking minors.

**One sentence:** "Cuando no sabes qué hacer, AkiPasa te da un plan." Secondary English: "Make plans, not endless searches."

## Marketing voice
Natural Spain Spanish, warm, energetic and concise, without forced slang or shouting. Real venue/date/price in cards, not generic hype. EN remains available. Lead with actual activities rather than the venue database, business software, subscriptions or XP. Preserve the orange pin as a familiar recognition anchor pending professional brand review; experiment with expressive typography, energetic editorial photography, kinetic transitions and deep dark/bright contrast without inaccessible text or animation overload. Respect reduced motion and battery/data constraints.

## Homepage priority (pilot)
1. Hero question **¿Qué hacemos hoy?** and one short promise.
2. One-tap **Hoy**, **Esta noche**, **Este finde**, **Gratis**, plus clear city switch. No login wall.
3. Actual verified future plans with dates, venue, neighborhood, price/free, age restrictions and honest freshness.
4. When empty, an honest nearby/date expansion and useful categories, not stock events or generic 75k-venue directory pretending to be active plans.
5. Save/share/WhatsApp/deep-link/directions as meaningful, measurable actions. Venue directory remains accessible as supporting search/SEO.

## Retention model
**Utility first, progress second, community third.** Measure weekly active planners, 7/28-day repeat meaningful planning, relevant plan found within 60 seconds, result freshness, event coverage, saves, shares and directions. Do not optimize raw screen time, daily login streaks, infinite scroll or notification pressure. A useful 90-second session leading to a real experience can be better than 20 minutes of wandering.

Passport and AkiPals become optional identity/progress after genuinely verified attendance, then 3–5 opt-in AkiQuests during a controlled venue pilot. Saved/directions = intent, never attendance XP. QR proof server-validated, rotated, tenant-authorized and replay resistant. No location history public by default, no spending-based perks, no unmoderated messaging/reviews.

## Distribution
Creator-friendly local event cards and WhatsApp/Instagram/TikTok links with accurate canonical event destinations. Small founding-venue cohort, staff-curated upcoming events and a weekly ES-first "Qué pasa este finde" editorial format. Do not fabricate events, fake scarcity or buy engagement. Use photo/video only with rights. Venue partners see genuine interest, not vanity impressions.

## Brand research and experiments
IAB Spain Social Media Study 2026: Gen Z 18–30 uses 6.1 social platforms a month, averages 1h23 daily; entertainment, creators and TikTok/Instagram/YouTube are significant discovery channels (https://iabspain.es/iab-spain-presenta-estudio-rrss-2026/). These describe external social habits, not proof that AkiPasa should mimic addictive feeds.

Run concept tests of 3 headlines and 2 card treatments with 18–35 adults in the pilot area. Check first-plan conversion, readability, speed, accessibility, consent-safe cohorts and qualitative recall. Keep a neutral control. Do not ship based only on watch time.

## Product boundaries and release gates
AkiDuermo/AkiCoches remain branded **under development / coming soon**, with only consent-based segmented waiting lists after tested verification and deletion. Avoid prominent navigation stealing attention from AkiPasa. AkiHQ is behind business login; AkiBusiness stays a separate operations product and site. Preserve returning users, auth and deep-links.

This change begins with reversible hero copy only. No billing, tracking, minors policy, DB changes, new analytics providers, marketing broadcasts or automatic deployment. Run lint, types, tests, build, browser/mobile and exact-source Cloudflare acceptance before merging. Next engineering PRs: event-supply editorial workflow and youth-oriented card refresh, each gated on real dated inventory.
