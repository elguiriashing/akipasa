# AkiPasa Brand and Retention Direction

28 October 2026. Initial launch focus: young adults aged 18–35 in Fuengirola, Mijas Costa and Benalmádena. Keep the product welcoming to all eligible ages.

## Promise

**¿Qué hacemos hoy?** Make it easy to discover a worthwhile local plan with friends, tonight or this weekend.

Lead with live music, local culture, free and low-cost plans, and genuine upcoming events. Do not position the consumer app as a nationwide business directory. Keep useful venue search and its existing canonical pages.

## Brand

Use the founder-approved orange movement mark as the visual reference, paired with near-black contrast, bold geometric type and expressive event photography where rights are cleared. Spanish-first, accessible bilingual copy; energetic without forced slang. Motion must respect reduced-motion preferences and constrained devices.

## App-first experience

Lead with Hoy, Esta noche, Este finde and Gratis. Give each genuine event a correct Europe/Madrid date, place, distance and price. Anonymous browsing should reach a useful plan within a minute. Keep honest empty states when there are no future events. Preserve event URLs, consent handling and app deep links.

## Retention

Prioritize weekly meaningful planners, successful plan discovery and return visits rather than raw screen time, infinite scroll or daily streak pressure. Allow sharing, saving and directions without unnecessary friction. Only venue-verified attendance should earn prestigious Passport and AkiPal progress. Start with three to five opt-in AkiQuests after supply and anti-fraud tests.

## Distribution

Build WhatsApp-friendly event links and optional social sharing, small creator collaborations and a weekly local weekend roundup. Recruit founding venues and verify actual upcoming events. Never fabricate content, sell unverified availability or send bulk messages without explicit consent.

## Product boundaries

AkiDuermo and AkiCoches remain separate under-development products with gated, verified waiting lists. AkiHQ remains business management; AkiBusiness is independent. Do not merge product features or billing.

## Release acceptance

This phase updates reversible ES/EN homepage copy only. Preserve tracking and age restrictions, tenant isolation and all existing browsing. Require full contract, formatting, browser, tablet, Cloudflare build and exact-source production checks before merge.
