# AkiPasa brand

- Wordmark and logo treatment: **AKIPASA**
- Normal prose: **AkiPasa**
- Domain and handles: **akipasa** / `akipasa.com`
- Primary lockup: **AKIPASA — Todo lo que pasa cerca de ti.**

The name carries two intended readings: “Aquí pasa” (this is where things happen) and “¿Qué pasa aquí?” (what is happening around here?). Spanish is the primary emotional expression; English copy should communicate the meaning naturally rather than explaining the pun every time.

Approved supporting lines:

- ¿Qué pasa hoy? AkiPasa.
- Where things happen.
- Descubre lo que pasa cerca.
- Aquí pasan cosas.
- What’s on near you.

Brand names remain centralized in configuration. Do not hard-code alternative spellings, imitate competitor identities, or claim formal trademark clearance. Domain purchase and legal clearance remain approval-gated launch tasks.
