# EU/Spain legal review pack

This is an engineering review pack, not legal advice or a claim of legal
approval. A qualified Spanish/EU lawyer must approve the final public wording
and operating process.

## Information the lawyer needs from the owner

- Legal controller name, company form, tax/NIF number, registered address,
  company-registry details and public contact details.
- Establishment country and intended launch countries.
- Whether AkiPasa contracts with consumers, businesses, or both, and whether it
  will ever take booking/payment money.
- Minimum user age and whether age-restricted events may be listed.
- Final retention periods for accounts, logs, reports, moderation evidence,
  rewards, business claims and deletion-request audit records.
- Named processors and regions: Supabase, Cloudflare, Resend, the selected map
  provider, Google OAuth and any support/analytics tools.
- Any non-EEA transfers and the applicable safeguards/DPA terms.
- Identity of the DPO or privacy contact, if applicable.

## GDPR review

- Replace the preview privacy summary with an Articles 12–14 notice covering
  controller identity, purposes, data categories, lawful basis per purpose,
  recipients/processors, international transfers, retention, data-subject
  rights, consent withdrawal, complaint rights and the competent supervisory
  authority.
- Validate contract/legitimate-interest/consent choices for account operation,
  fraud prevention, location, product analytics, emails and business reporting.
- Approve the account export and deletion workflow, including which audit or
  financial records must be retained and the lawful basis/period.
- Complete a record of processing activities, processor register, breach
  procedure and data-subject request procedure. Assess whether a DPIA is needed.
- Review minors, precise/coarse location, profiling, rewards and sponsored
  ranking.

## Spanish digital-service and consumer review

- Confirm the required LSSI-CE legal notice and electronic-contact disclosures.
- Review consumer information, external bookings, pricing, promotions/prizes,
  advertising labels and business verification.
- Determine AkiPasa's Digital Services Act classification and any small/micro
  enterprise exemptions.
- Review illegal-content notice-and-action, decision notices, internal appeals,
  abuse suspension rules, contact point and content-moderation transparency
  reporting.
- Review image/IP licences, user warranties, repeat infringement, venue claims,
  takedowns and preservation of evidence.

## Cookies and tracking

- Maintain a complete browser/storage inventory.
- Essential authentication and security storage should be documented.
- Do not enable non-essential analytics, advertising or map-provider tracking
  before the lawyer confirms whether prior consent and a consent platform are
  required.
- Recheck this inventory whenever a provider or SDK changes.

## Approval record

Record reviewer name/firm, jurisdiction, review date, approved document version,
required changes and next review date. Change `currentTermsVersion` only after
the approved text is deployed; existing accounts will then be asked to accept
the new version.
