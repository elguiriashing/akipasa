# Claim decision emails and early-partner onboarding (27 October 2026)

## Actual source of truth

The transactional approval and rejection emails are generated by `src/lib/claim-decision-email.ts`, dispatched via `src/lib/claim-mail-delivery.ts` and persisted in `public.claim_decision_emails`. These are **not** Resend-hosted templates. Claim moderation is in `src/app/[locale]/staff/ClaimReview.tsx` and `src/app/[locale]/moderation/actions.ts`.

The mandatory **Decision reason** box is written verbatim into the applicant's email and to the claim audit. It is not an additional private or optional marketing message. Type a factual and courteous verification note (e.g., "Business email and phone matched the venue's verified details"); on denial, specify the missing evidence and what the applicant must do. Standard welcome, remediation, survey and onboarding sections are supplied automatically.

The separate "Request more information" control opens a mailto draft. It does not change claim status or go through Resend.

## Approved claim

The existing bilingual approval email keeps ownership confirmation, decision notes, starter checklist and current venue management link. The new early-partner section explains that AkiHQ/business tools remain in development, invites feedback and links to an authenticated claim-specific form at `https://akipasa.com/{locale}/business/early-partner?claim={claimId}`.

The form asks where the owner heard about AkiPasa (Google Search, Google Maps, AkiPasa listing, social media, personal recommendation, direct outreach, other) and their most useful business need (listing, events, bookings, marketing, AkiHQ operations, other), plus optional free text and **separate optional permission to contact about tailored onboarding**. Feedback is private, not public review content and not marketing opt-in.

## Early-partner gift

The invitation offers **three months of AkiPasa business listing Premium** for the approved venue, subject to manual eligibility confirmation, without a payment card or automatic renewal. The message must **never** falsely claim an entitlement was already activated.

The database migration `20261027031500_claim_early_partner_feedback.sql` creates `claim_owner_feedback` and a restricted `claim_partner_rewards` queue. It only accepts the actual signed-in claimant of an _approved_ claim. The reward row is inserted once, on first submission, in state `pending`. It does not edit Stripe, subscriptions, plan entitlements or payment settings.

A staff operator should monitor the pending queue, check current listing plan and any previous gifts, offer the tailored onboarding, apply the gift through the verified, existing entitlement administration path, and only then mark the reward `granted` with a confirmed end date. If incompatible with existing subscription or listing rules, mark `ineligible`, explain clearly and provide an appropriate alternative. Never change ownership or grant paid operating-plan features under this offer. Staff must not manually modify billing records without testing the actual entitlement path.

## Rejected claim

The denial stays purely transactional: the actual reason, evidence correction steps, resubmission link and support response. **No Premium or early-partner pitch on rejection.**
